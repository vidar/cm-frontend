- **Chess pieces:** the "cburnett" set by Colin M.L. Burnett, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:SVG_chess_pieces), used under the BSD 3-Clause license (one of the licenses it is offered under).
- **Daily puzzles:** selected from the [Lichess puzzle database](https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles), released into the public domain (CC0). Each puzzle links to the game it came from.
- **Openings:** the [Lichess chess-openings dataset](https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings), CC0.
- **Opening statistics and example games:** computed from the [Lichess open database](https://database.lichess.org/) of rated games (CC0); engine evaluations from the Lichess evaluations database (CC0).
- **Games database:** over-the-board games from [The Week in Chess](https://theweekinchess.com/) (issues from 2012 onwards), © Mark Crowther, used with permission. Each game links to the TWIC issue it came from. Please support TWIC.
- **Engine:** [Stockfish](https://stockfishchess.org/) 19 via [Stockfish.js](https://github.com/nmrugg/stockfish.js) (lite WebAssembly build), GPLv3 — runs unmodified in your browser on the [analysis board](/analysis/); see [source and licence](/engine/README.txt).
- **Move generation:** [chess.js](https://github.com/jhlywa/chess.js) (BSD 2-Clause).
- **Fonts:** [Fraunces](https://github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces) and [Inter](https://github.com/rsms/inter), SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **AI notes** on game pages are written by [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/claude) (Anthropic) from the stored Stockfish analysis.
- Built with [Astro](https://astro.build) and hosted on [Cloudflare Workers](https://workers.cloudflare.com).

The full licence texts of chess.js, the pieces and the fonts are on the [licences](/licences/) page. What the site stores about visitors, organisers and players is explained in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).
